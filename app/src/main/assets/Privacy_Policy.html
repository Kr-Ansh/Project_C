Privacy Policy

Project C — Last updated: August 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Project C ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile game. By using Project C you agree to the terms described here.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary for the game to function and to improve the player experience. This may include:

  • Usage Data: gameplay progress, achievements, chapter completions, hint usage, and device information (device type, OS version, unique device identifiers).
  • Saved Game Data: your puzzle progress and local settings needed to run the game.
  • Optional Social Login Data: if you choose to log in using a third-party service (e.g., Google), we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email. This is optional.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as contacts, payment details, or precise location unless you explicitly provide it for a gameplay purpose.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data only for the following purposes:

  • Saving and loading your game progress.
  • Unlocking in-game features such as optional hints and chapter completions.
  • Providing optional rewarded hints via ads (ad networks may use identifiers for ad delivery).
  • Analyzing gameplay to improve puzzles, fix bugs, and optimize performance.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Sharing

We may share limited, non-identifiable data with:

  • Service Providers who help operate the game (for example analytics or ad networks used for rewarded ads).
  • Legal authorities if required to do so by law.

Third-party providers are required to handle your data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention & Reset

Your game data is stored locally on your device or in app-managed storage only for as long as needed for gameplay. When you complete the final chapter the game will reset your progress and delete stored game data. You can also request manual deletion by contacting us.

5. Children’s Privacy

Project C is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete that information.

6. Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our game or legal requirements. We will publish the updated policy here with a revised "Last updated" date.
